Teacher note: The source records and online posts used here are fictional training examples, not authentic Holocaust documents or testimony. For a lesson using real historical evidence, replace them with verified sources from a museum or archive. The contemporary scenarios explore methods of manipulation; they do not equate present-day online content with the Holocaust.

Think like a historian

Ask: Who created this source? When? For what purpose? What does it show? What is missing? Can another reliable source confirm it?

Historical connection

Nazi antisemitic propaganda used stereotypes, repetition, emotional appeals and misleading claims as part of a wider system of exclusion, discriminatory laws, state policy and persecution. No single technique alone explains the Holocaust. Studying propaganda requires both close analysis of messages and an accurate understanding of their historical context.
