For educators

Use the activities as a starting point for discussion.

Each activity is designed to take approximately 8–12 minutes individually, or 12–15 minutes in pairs. Follow it with a classroom conversation that invites questions, careful reasoning and respectful listening.

Read teacher notes  ↓

Before the lesson

  • Review the activity and its learning objectives.
  • Set expectations for respectful discussion.
  • Make space for students to ask questions or pause.
  • When using real historical documents, verify their provenance and context.
Important: The interactive activities use clearly labelled summaries based on USHMM historical resources. They link to the original context and do not present adapted summaries as verbatim quotations. Contemporary information-literacy scenarios are not presented as equivalent to the Holocaust.
