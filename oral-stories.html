How to listen like a historian

Choose a short excerpt. Listen once without taking notes. Listen again and notice: What detail stays with you? What does the speaker actually say? What would you need to check in another source?

LISTEN CLOSELY

How to listen like a historian

Who is speaking, and when was the testimony recorded?
Is this a memory, an observation, an interpretation or information learned later?
What does the testimony help us understand?
What can it not tell us on its own? What other source could help?
A testimony is a personal account, not a complete record of every event. Listen with respect. Do not imitate accents, turn trauma into a game, or assume one person speaks for everyone. Some recordings describe persecution and violence.
